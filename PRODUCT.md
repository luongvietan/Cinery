# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Filmmakers, directors, visual storytellers, and advanced AI creators who manage cinematic projects across multiple generation models. They work in long-lived, local-first productions where Canon, characters, assets, workflow history, approvals, versions, and provenance must remain coherent across sessions.

## Product Purpose

AI Cinematic Production OS is a local-first production environment for moving work through a clear authority chain: Project → Canon → Assets → Workflow → Approval → Output. Success means creators can understand what is authoritative, what changed, why a workflow is waiting, what was approved, and which exact context produced an output without relying on chat history or hidden model behavior.

## Brand Personality

Cinematic · Precise · Restrained

The product should feel visually sophisticated and appropriate for professional filmmakers without becoming decorative. State, version, Canon authority, workflow progress, approval, and provenance must be immediately legible. The interface stays quiet as projects and workflows become complex.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a cyberpunk control panel, gaming or crypto dashboard, generic AI chatbot, neon-purple AI product, generic SaaS admin template, ComfyUI node graph, literal Adobe Premiere clone, or glossy glassmorphism showcase. Avoid excessive gradients, glow, particles, animated decoration, and “AI magic” language. Do not turn every surface into a card; use production-oriented workspaces, panels, inspectors, grids, and lists.

## Design Principles

1. Content before state, state before action, action before decoration.
2. Make authority visible: locked Canon and canonical Assets should read as dependable production facts.
3. Separate approval from execution and make destructive or Canon-changing actions explicit.
4. Preserve provenance: versions, snapshots, transitions, and outputs remain inspectable after restart.
5. Prefer calm density: complex workflows may contain substantial information, but hierarchy and standard affordances keep them understandable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast. Support full keyboard navigation, visible `:focus-visible` states, reduced motion, browser zoom and text scaling, and practical 40–44 px targets for important actions. Status must always combine text with a non-color cue. Use semantic landmarks and labels, explicit form labels, associated validation messages, focus-managed dialogs, non-hover-only actions, and meaningful asset names. Restrained styling must not become low-contrast gray-on-gray UI.
